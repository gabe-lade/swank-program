About this estimate

An estimate for tax year 2025 bills, paid in 2026. For most spots, the rates are that spot's taxing district's 2025 effective residential rates, after the state's reductions that offset rising values (HB 920). For a spot marked approx., it uses each government's typical (median) rate, because the spot could not be matched to one district. The state's 10% and 2.5% credits apply only to levies approved before late 2013. The 10% covers homes and farmland, and owner-occupied homes get 2.5% more, so across a whole bill they come to roughly 10% (the real Hilliard bill, an owner-occupied home, nets about 9%). The estimate applies a flat 10% to any home's 2025 bill; a rental got the 10% part but not the extra 2.5%, so its credit is a bit smaller than shown. From tax year 2026 the 10% credit phases out over four years, reaching zero for rentals by 2029. Unticked, the estimate is the bill before any state credit.
