About this estimate

An estimate for tax year 2025 bills, paid in 2026. For a matched spot, the rates are that spot's taxing district's 2025 effective residential rates after HB 920; for a spot marked approx., each government's typical (median) rate, because the spot could not be matched to one district. The state credits are applied as a flat share (about 10%) rather than levy by levy. Unticked, the estimate is the bill before any state credit. In 2025 rental homes still received the 10% credit, which phases out from 2026.
