How far plants are from their next line (not public)

Two tests, from the same arithmetic as the bars above: the plants whose 50-mile circles cover the county would fall short of a full step from the documented corn around them if half of the qualifying corn is documented (corn no-till at half the Census rate, cover crops as in the central case, and no plant using more documented corn than it buys), and more than one company buys corn there. The switch compares plants that need a full 5-point step with plants halfway to their next line, which need half as much; nobody outside a plant knows which is closer to the truth. Plants owned by the same company count as one buyer, and two companies counted in a county may not both be within reach of every farm in it; Indiana and Michigan plants within 100 miles of Ohio are included. Hatched counties are mostly more than 50 miles from any plant; under USDA's rules for tracking documented grain their documented corn counts only if it reaches a plant through buyers that keep records.
